The division of labour

INFERENCE AT THE EDGE, AGGREGATION IN THE CLOUD

On the phone

  • Image classification, text recognition, embedding
  • Duplicate search over this crew's data
  • Evidence hashing at capture
  • Every read and write the app makes

Fixed cost. Works with no signal. Nothing leaves the device until it syncs.

In Capella

  • The system of record, across every district
  • Access control, in the sync function
  • Cross-district questions no single phone can answer
  • Analytics, alerting, retention, integration

The work that is genuinely global, and only that.

This split is the architecture, not an optimisation. It is also the answer to "why not just call a cloud API": the moment capture depends on a round trip, beat one is gone.
