The field reality

THREE PROBLEMS THAT SHOW UP TOGETHER

No signal where the work is

Canyons, basements, tunnels, rural spans. The app either works there or the report is written twice: once on paper, once later from memory.

The same problem, reported five times

Four crews photograph one pothole in a week. Someone triages the duplicates by hand, or four work orders go out.

Evidence that has to hold up

A photograph in a dispute is only worth what you can say about where it came from and whether it changed.

FieldProof answers all three on the device, then syncs. Nothing in the first four minutes of this demo needs a network.

Anchor the demo in the customer’s world before showing software. Ask which of the three they feel most — the answer tells you which slide to spend time on later.
