Where a real deployment goes next

WHAT WE WOULD ADD FOR YOU

Your own model

A Core ML classifier trained with Create ML on your photographs and your categories, replacing the generic labels.

Your identity provider

OIDC sign-in, per-person identity, district access from group claims — instead of the demo's user picker.

Crew-to-crew sync

Couchbase Lite 4.1 replicates directly between nearby phones over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, with no infrastructure at all.

Capella AI Data Plane

On a paid cluster: summaries, triage and PII masking in SQL++, and vectors for photos from other channels. Search-service vectors for duplicates across districts.

Stronger provenance

Sign the hash with a Secure Enclave key and attest the app, so the hash itself cannot be quietly rewritten.

Bandwidth control

Delta sync, and push filters that hold full-resolution photographs until the phone is on Wi-Fi.

Pick two that match what the customer said in the first five minutes. Every item here is a documented path, not a wish: the architecture notes in the repository cover each one with trade-offs. The AI Data Plane card is a review we have not run yet: it needs a paid cluster (Couchbase Server 8.0+, Developer Pro or Enterprise support), and server-side vectors only compare with the phones’ if one model makes both.
